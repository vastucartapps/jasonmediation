When a Repair Counts as an Emergency

Three situations put you in emergency territory: the unit is dead and won't start at all, it runs but only pushes warm air through the vents, or it keeps tripping the breaker every time it cycles on. None of those are "wait until Thursday" problems once the house starts heating up. The diagnostic work itself doesn't change — a technician still checks the same components — but a same-day slot gets prioritized when one is open.

Still getting some cooling, just weaker than it used to be? That's a standard-schedule situation, not an emergency one. The Maricopa County AC Repair page covers that kind of visit.
